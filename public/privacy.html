SideBet

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 June 2026
The short version: SideBet is an entertainment app for running friendly betting pools — it does not involve real-money gambling, payments, or payouts. We don't ask for accounts, we don't collect personal information, and we don't show ads or sell data.

What this app is

SideBet lets a host run pari-mutuel "pools" for an event (a race, a tournament, a cook-off) and lets guests place pretend wagers. All stakes and payouts are virtual and for fun only. No real currency changes hands through the app.

Information we handle

  • Display name. When you place a bet, you choose a display name. It's stored locally on your device so your bets show up under you. It is sent to the host's server only as a label attached to the bets you place.
  • Bets you place. The contestant, pool, and amount of each bet are stored on the server that the host is running, so the pool and results can be calculated.
  • That's it. No email, no phone number, no password, no payment details, no location, no contacts, no device identifiers for tracking.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No account system and no cross-app or cross-site tracking.

Where data lives

SideBet is self-hosted: each host runs their own server instance, and event data (contestants, pools, bets) is stored there. The host controls that data and can clear or delete an event at any time, which removes its bets. Your display name also lives in your own browser/app storage and you can clear it from your device at any time.

Children

SideBet simulates betting and is intended for a general/teen-and-up audience as rated on the app stores. It contains no real-money gambling. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
