## Full description (Google Play max 4000 / App Store description)

**Turn any event into a betting pool.**

SideBet lets you run friendly, real-time betting pools for absolutely anything — a dog race, a golf tournament, a fantasy draft, a chili cook-off, who's-late-to-the-meeting. Pick your contestants, open the pools, and watch the odds move as everyone gets their bets in. No real money, no bookie — just the fun part.

It's **pari-mutuel**, like a racetrack: every bet goes into a shared pool, and when the result's in, whoever backed the winner splits the pool. SideBet does the math and the live odds for you.

**Built for any game:**
• Winner-takes-pool — back who comes first
• Top-N share — pick anyone to land in the top 2, 3, or more
• Head-to-head — go one-on-one on a single matchup
• Run as many pools at once as you want

**Start in seconds with templates:**
🏁 Race Day · ⛳ Tournament · 🥊 Head-to-Head · ✨ build your own

**Why people love it:**
• Live odds that move with every bet
• A clean, fast card-based interface
• See your bets, your net, and a live odds board
• No accounts — guests just pick a name and play
• A host console to run the whole event from your phone

SideBet is for entertainment. There's no real-money wagering and no payouts — it's all for the bragging rights.
